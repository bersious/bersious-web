04 · TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY

Thanatechnology Safeguards

Aeterna addresses the core ethical dilemmas of grief tech by enforcing five unbreachable guardrails.

⚖️ Explicit Non-Biological Framing

The agent never pretends to be a biological immortal soul. It consistently disclaims its nature as a lovingly aligned digital legacy, completely avoiding psychological manipulation.

⏳ Anti-Dependency Circuit Breaker

To prevent pathological grief or unhealthy emotional escapism, daily interaction limits are enforced on descendants, gently guiding bereaved relatives towards real-world human support.

🔑 Cryptographic Digital Will

The user retains full sovereignty while alive. Posthumous stewardship permissions, topic blacklists, and secret seals can only be authorized via personal cryptographic keys.

🛡️ Zero Hallucination Guarantee

Answers regarding personal beliefs, family secrets, or past commitments are strictly retrieved from verifiable vector graphs. If unrecorded, the agent transparently states lack of knowledge.
